How it works

Three steps. Two minutes.

If you can print a piece of paper, you can run a NextUp queue.

🖼️
STEP 1

Print your QR code

One QR code per business, generated the moment you sign up. Print it as a window poster or a table tent.

📱
STEP 2

Customers join in seconds

Scan → enter a name → get a number. Optional phone number for a text when it's almost their turn. No login, ever.

📣
STEP 3

Call the next person

Your dashboard shows the whole line. One tap moves someone to "Now Serving" and their screen updates instantly.

Everything else you need

Small tool. Does the whole job.

⏱️

Live wait estimates

Customers see their position and an honest wait time, updating in real time.

💬

Text alerts

Optional SMS when someone is two away from the front. They come back on time.

🏪

Multiple counters

Counter 1, Counter 2, barber chair, exam room — each staff member runs their own line.

📊

Owner analytics

Average wait, daily volume, and your busiest hour. Know your rush before it hits.
